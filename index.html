Privacy Policy for Venpa

Effective date: 7 August 2026
App: Venpa (package name: venpa.com)
Contact: devivaraprasad144@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Venpa (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the Android app for shop billing, inventory, and related features.

1. Summary

  • Venpa is designed for on-device shop use.
  • Your inventory, bills, customers, and photos are stored locally on your phone.
  • We use Google Sign-In so you can sign in to the app.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not show ads in the app.

2. Information we process

A. Google account information (for sign-in)

  • Google account identifier
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo URL (if provided by Google)

This information is obtained through Google Sign-In / Credential Manager and stored on your device to keep you signed in. It is used only for authentication and account display in the app.

B. Shop and business data you enter

  • Shop name, address, GSTIN/GST number, and phone number
  • Inventory item details (name, price, HSN, GST rate, stock, unit)
  • Item photos you capture for product recognition
  • Invoices / bills, payment mode, discounts, and related totals
  • Customer names, phone numbers, and udhaar (credit) balances
  • Reports generated from your local billing history

C. Device permissions

  • Camera — to photograph products for cataloging and bill matching
  • Internet — for Google Sign-In

The app may open system share sheets (for example PDF share / WhatsApp) when you choose to share an invoice. Content you share is controlled by you and handled by the app you select.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your session on the device
  • To create bills, manage inventory, customers, and reports
  • To match product photos using on-device AI / image features
  • To generate and share invoice PDFs when you request it
  • To export or restore a local backup when you choose Backup / Restore

4. Where data is stored

Business data (items, photos, invoices, customers, shop details) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage/databases. Sign-in profile details are stored in on-device preferences.

Venpa does not operate a Venpa cloud server that uploads your inventory, customer list, or invoices for storage. If you use Google Sign-In, Google processes authentication under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

5. Backup and restore

If you use Export Backup, the app creates a local file (JSON) that may include shop, inventory, invoice, and customer information. You choose where to save it (for example Google Drive, Downloads, or another folder). Anyone with access to that file can read the exported data, so store backups securely.

6. On-device AI / photo matching

Product photo matching runs on your device using an on-device model. Photos used for matching are processed locally for the purpose of identifying catalog items during billing.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your shop/customer data with advertisers.

Limited sharing may occur only when:

  • You sign in with Google (authentication with Google)
  • You choose to share an invoice/PDF through another app
  • You choose to export a backup file to a location you select
  • Required by law or to protect rights and safety

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Signing out removes the stored Google sign-in session from the app.
  • Uninstalling Venpa removes local app data from the device (unless you previously exported a backup).

9. Children’s privacy

Venpa is intended for business / shop use and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

10. Security

We use standard Android on-device storage practices. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Protect your phone with a screen lock and keep backup files private.

11. Your choices

  • You can refuse camera permission (some features will not work).
  • You can sign out of Google from Settings.
  • You can delete customers/items/bills in the app where available.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove local app data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
